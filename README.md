## 📌 Project Overview

This is a beginner-level **Sales Data Analysis** practice project using Power BI. The dashboard summarizes sales, profit, order volume, product performance, category performance, regional profit, and monthly sales.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key sales and profit KPIs.
- Compare sales across product categories and months.
- Review profit contribution by region.
- Explore product-level performance using dashboard visuals and filters.
- Practice building an interactive business dashboard in Power BI.

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

- **Total Sales by Product** — compares sales for products such as Bluetooth Speaker, Printer, Ergonomic Chair, Laptop Sleeve, and Storage Box.
- **Total Profit by Region** — compares regional profit contribution.
- **Total Sales by Category** — compares Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
- **Total Sales by Month** — displays month-wise sales trends.
- **Interactive filters** — Region, Category, Segment, and Order Date.

## 🧰 Tools & Skills

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — KPI measures and calculations
- **Data Analysis** — sales performance analysis and business reporting

## 🔍 Business Questions

1. What are the overall sales, profit, order count, and profit percentage?
2. Which products contribute the most to sales?
3. How do sales compare across categories?
4. Which regions contribute to total profit?
5. How does sales performance vary by month?
6. How do results change when Region, Category, Segment, or Order Date filters are applied?
