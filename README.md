## 📌 Project Overview

This is a beginner-level **Sales Data Analysis** practice project created to explore sales performance and build an interactive dashboard using Power BI. It helps summarize key business metrics and identify patterns in sales and profit.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track key metrics such as total sales, profit, orders, and profit margin (where available).
- Compare performance across product categories, regions, and time periods (depending on the dataset).
- Present findings in a clear, interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🧰 Tools & Skills

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard design and data visualization
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — measures and calculated metrics
- **Data Analysis** — KPI tracking and business insights

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard is intended to summarize business performance through KPI cards and visualizations.

Suggested key metrics:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

## 🔍 Analysis Questions

1. Which categories or products generate the most sales?
2. Which segments or regions contribute most to profit?
3. How do sales and profit change over time?
4. Are there categories with high sales but low profit?
